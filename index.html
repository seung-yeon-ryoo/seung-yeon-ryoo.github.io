Seung-Yeon Ryoo

Hausdorff Center for Mathematics
University of Bonn

I am an incoming postdoctoral researcher at the Hausdorff Center for Mathematics at the University of Bonn. From 2023 to 2026 I was a postdoctoral teaching fellow in the Department of Mathematics at the California Institute of Technology. I received my Ph.D. in 2023 from Princeton University under the supervision of Assaf Naor.

CV

Research interests

  • Metric geometry of nilpotent Lie groups.
  • Bilipschitz and snowflake embeddings of metric spaces into Euclidean and Lp spaces.
  • Mathematical models of synchronization, with an emphasis on the Winfree and Kuramoto models.
