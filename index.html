Seung-Yeon Ryoo

The Division of Physics, Mathematics and Astronomy
California Institute of Technology

I am a postdoctoral teaching fellow at the Mathematics Department of the California Institute of Technology. Previously, I received my Ph.D. in 2023 at Princeton University under the supervision of Professor Assaf Naor. Starting in September 2026, I will be a postdoctoral researcher at the Hausdorff Center for Mathematics at the University of Bonn.

CV

Research Interests.

  • Metric geometry of nilpotent Lie groups.
  • Bilipschitz and snowflake embeddings of metric spaces into Euclidean and L^p spaces.
  • Mathematical models of synchronization, with an emphasis on the Winfree and Kuramoto models.
